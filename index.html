How relay works

From your senses to theirs, and back.

STEP 01

Sensory uplink

Visual, auditory, tactile, olfactory, and gustatory signals are intercepted at the array before reaching upper cortical processing.

STEP 02

Zero-latency relay

Signal is transmitted to a paired array in a second participant over a dedicated low-latency relay channel.

STEP 03

Synchronized integration

The receiving array delivers the incoming signal to the paired participant's own cortex as native sensory and motor input.
