⚠ WARNING — READ BEFORE PROCEEDING

All beta testers must be aware of the following before initiating a relay swap:

Once a swap has been initiated and consummated — meaning both paired participants are stably receiving one another's sensory and motor input — the timing of decoupling and differentiation is dependent on a number of physiological and neurological factors, and is not always predictable.

  • Nano Relay Solutions does not recommend initiating a swap for a duration of less than twenty-four (24) hours.
  • Certain intense physiological or emotional events experienced during a swap — including but not limited to severe sneezing fits, powerful yawning, uncontrollable laughter (including tickling-induced), intense crying, prolonged hiccups, or sexual climax — can delay decoupling by an additional seventy-two (72) to ninety-six (96) hours.
  • NOTE: Male participants are strongly encouraged to avoid any risk of experiencing female orgasm or any form of intense sexual arousal if swapped into a female body as data suggests certain males may become cognitively trapped in the new body for 3-6 months
  • Beta participants must be prepared to accommodate such delays before initiating any swap, and should plan accordingly.

By proceeding with this application, you confirm that you have read and understood this notice.
